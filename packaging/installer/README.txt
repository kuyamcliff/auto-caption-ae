CyriqCap 1.0.0 - Auto captions and text animations for After Effects
=====================================================================

Requirements
  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  After Effects 2019 (16.0) or newer

Install
  1. Close After Effects.
  2. Move the CyriqCap-API folder somewhere permanent,
     for example Documents\CyriqCap-API.
  3. Double-click "Install CyriqCap.bat".
     It installs the panel and the fonts for your Windows user.
     No admin rights are needed.
  4. Open After Effects > Window > Extensions > CyriqCap.
  5. Click "Choose API Folder" and select the CyriqCap-API folder.
     Every file is checked once. Click Continue when it finishes.

Use
  Captions tab  Select an audio, video or precomp layer, click
                Generate Captions, edit the text, style it, then click
                Create Text Layers.
  Animate tab   Select one or more text layers, pick a preset, click Apply.
  Help tab      Step-by-step guide with pictures.
  Settings tab  API folder, models, fonts and plug-ins.

More models
  Small is included. Tiny, Base, Medium and Large Turbo can be downloaded
  from the panel (download icon next to Model, or Settings > Models).
  They are saved in your CyriqCap-API folder.

Presets that use third-party plug-ins
  Deep Glow, Noise Emboss, Texture Spots, Texture Cells and Blood Texture
  need Deep Glow, Sapphire or BCC. CyriqCap tells you if one is missing and
  does not change your project.

Uninstall
  Double-click "Uninstall CyriqCap.bat".

Privacy
  Audio is transcribed on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.
